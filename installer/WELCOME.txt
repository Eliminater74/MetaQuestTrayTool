Welcome to Meta Quest Tray Tool Setup

This installs a Windows tray utility for Meta Quest Link / Air Link and SteamVR OpenXR.

Inspired by ApollyonVR's classic Oculus Tray Tool (OTT).

What you get:
- Global VR defaults + per-game profiles (auto-apply / restore)
- Quest Link bitrate & sharpening, OpenXR Meta vs SteamVR switch
- Audio / power helpers and SideQuest-style Quest ADB tweaks
- Bundled ADB — no Android Studio required

Requirements:
- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
- Meta Quest PC software and/or SteamVR for the features you use

This installer is self-contained (includes the .NET 8 runtime).
Your settings stay in %AppData%\MetaQuestTrayTool and are kept if you uninstall.
